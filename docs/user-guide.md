# ✉️ User guide

1. In **Gmail on the web**, click the add-on icon on the right edge (or the icon at the bottom of a new email).
2. Open a **category** and pick a template.
3. **Preview** shows it on desktop and mobile.
4. **Customise** opens your own copy in a new tab. Click text to retype it, or a photo to swap it. Locked parts stay as they are. Click **Use in my email**.
5. Back in Gmail, click **Add to email**. A new email opens with your template, or it goes into the email you're writing.

> Your customised copy is kept for 24 hours, and you have one at a time. The original template never changes.
