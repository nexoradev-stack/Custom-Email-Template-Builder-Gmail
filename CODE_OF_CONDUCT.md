# 💜 Code of Conduct

We want this project to be friendly and welcoming for everyone.

- Be respectful and kind, especially when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and give helpful, specific feedback.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect people's privacy: never share someone's personal details.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through GitHub.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
